Section 1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Youtube Unsubscribe All (“the Extension”) handles your information when you use it to manage your YouTube subscriptions. The Extension works entirely inside your browser. Your YouTube data is never sent to the developer, sold or shared.

Section 2Information We Access

  • Your subscriptions: on youtube.com, the Extension reads the channels you are subscribed to, such as each channel’s name, picture and subscriber count, so it can show them and unsubscribe only the channels you choose.
  • Channel activity (optional): if you use the quiet-channel finder, the Extension opens each channel’s public videos page on YouTube to see when its latest video was posted.
  • How it works: the Extension works in your open YouTube tab, where you are already signed in, and unsubscribes by using YouTube’s own buttons. It never asks for, reads or stores your password.
  • License key: if you buy Premium, the key you enter is used only to confirm your purchase.

Section 3How We Use Information

We use information only to provide the Extension’s features:

  • Showing your channels and unsubscribing the ones you choose, while always skipping the channels you keep.
  • Finding channels that have gone quiet, if you ask it to.
  • Keeping a history so you can undo a removal.

Everything happens on your own device, in your browser.

Section 4Where Your Data Is Kept

Your settings, kept channels, selected channels, removal progress and removal history are saved only in your browser. You can clear the history at any time.

A free-use counter is also saved in Chrome’s sync storage, which Chrome may copy to your other browsers signed in to the same Google account. Data saved by the Extension is deleted when you uninstall it.

Section 5Who We Share Data With

We do not share your data with anyone, with one exception: checking your license. When you enter a license key, it is sent with the product ID to Gumroad, our payment provider, only to confirm that it is valid. Payments are handled by Gumroad, and the Extension never sees your payment details.

Section 6Things We Never Do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or credit decisions.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than managing your YouTube subscriptions.
  • We do not track your activity or load code from outside the Extension.

Section 7Children

The Extension is not intended for children.

Section 8Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
